Phone Auto Format - CRM

Auto-format phone numbers on CRM leads and opportunities.

Requires Phone Auto Format. This add-on applies the same format chosen in Settings → Phone Auto Format to the phone and mobile fields of leads and opportunities. It installs automatically when both CRM and Phone Auto Format are installed.
