Consistent contact details across your sales pipeline

Leads and opportunities can come from your team, website forms, imports or integrations. This add-on extends Phone Auto Format to Odoo CRM, applying the phone format selected in your central settings to CRM phone and mobile fields.

What you get

The same formatting rules are applied throughout the CRM lead and opportunity workflow.

01   Leads and opportunities

Keeps contact numbers consistent on CRM records as leads progress through your sales pipeline.

02   Phone and mobile fields

Applies your selected format to the phone and mobile numbers stored on CRM leads and opportunities.

03   Multiple ways to create leads

Formatting also applies when CRM records are created through web forms, imports and API calls, subject to the base module's processing.

04   No separate configuration

The add-on uses the format already selected in Phone Auto Format settings. There is no second set of CRM formatting options to maintain.

One setting. Consistent CRM numbers.

Uses your Phone Auto Format settings

Choose the preferred number format once in Settings > Phone Auto Format. The CRM add-on extends that configuration to lead and opportunity phone details, so your sales team does not need to manage formatting separately.

Supported formats

The CRM add-on follows the format configured in the main Phone Auto Format module.

International Readable international number with its country prefix.
E.164 Standard international representation, typically beginning with + and containing digits.
National Number displayed in the national format for its country.
RFC 3966 Telephone number represented using the RFC 3966 tel URI format.

Simple installation

01

Install the base module

Install the free Phone Auto Format module to provide the core formatting behavior and settings.

02

Install or enable CRM

Install or enable the Odoo CRM application. The CRM add-on installs automatically when its required modules are installed.

03

Choose your format

Open Settings > Phone Auto Format and select the preferred number format. CRM uses that same setting.

Required: the free Phone Auto Format base module and Odoo CRM. The number format is configured centrally in Settings > Phone Auto Format.

Good to know

• The CRM add-on extends the formatting behavior provided by the main Phone Auto Format module.
• Phone and mobile fields on CRM leads and opportunities use the centrally selected format.
• Designed for Odoo 20.0. The add-on is free and is intended for Community and Enterprise editions, subject to the base module's requirements.
