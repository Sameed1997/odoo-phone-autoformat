Phone Auto Format

CRM

Clean phone numbers on every lead and opportunity.

Odoo 19Free add-onAuto-installs

This add-on applies the same phone format you chose in Settings to the phone and mobile fields of your CRM leads and opportunities.

What you get

Leads and opportunities
Numbers are formatted when a lead is edited or saved, including leads created by web forms, imports and the API.
Install and forget
It installs by itself when both CRM and Phone Auto Format are installed. Nothing to configure.
Requires the free Phone Auto Format module. The format is chosen in Settings → Phone Auto Format.
