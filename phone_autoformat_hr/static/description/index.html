Employee phone details, one consistent format

Employee records can contain several types of phone numbers, entered by different people and in different formats. This add-on extends Phone Auto Format to Odoo Employees, applying your selected formatting rules to employee contact details.

What gets formatted

Coverage for the key phone details maintained on employee records.

01   Work phone

Keeps an employee's office or business phone number in the format selected in Phone Auto Format settings.

02   Work mobile

Applies the same formatting rules to the employee's business mobile number.

03   Private phone

Formats the personal phone number recorded on the employee profile.

04   Emergency contact phone

Keeps the emergency contact number consistent with the rest of the employee phone data.

Uses the employee's company country

A more reliable country fallback

When a phone number is entered without a country code, the add-on uses the employee's company country to interpret it. It does not use the employee's nationality, which may be different from the country associated with the phone number.

Works with your existing settings

The Employees add-on extends the formatting behavior provided by the main Phone Auto Format module. Choose your preferred number format once in Odoo Settings and the employee phone fields follow that configuration.

International Readable international number with its country prefix.
E.164 Standard international representation, typically beginning with + and containing digits.
National Number displayed in the national format for its country.
RFC 3966 Telephone number represented using the RFC 3966 tel URI format.

Simple installation

01

Install the base module

Install the free Phone Auto Format module, which provides the formatting settings and core behavior.

02

Use Employees

Install or enable the Odoo Employees application. This add-on installs automatically when both required modules are installed.

03

Choose your format

Open Settings > Phone Auto Format and select the number format for your database.

Required: the free Phone Auto Format base module. The number format is configured centrally in Settings > Phone Auto Format.

Good to know

• The formatting rules come from the main Phone Auto Format module; this add-on extends them to employee records.
• For numbers without a country code, the employee's company country is used instead of the employee's nationality.
• Designed for Odoo 16.0. The add-on is free and is intended to work with the Community and Enterprise editions, subject to the base module's requirements.
