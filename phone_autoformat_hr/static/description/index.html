Phone Auto Format - Employees

Auto-format phone numbers on employee records.

Requires Phone Auto Format. This add-on formats the work phone, work mobile, private phone and emergency contact phone of employees. Numbers are parsed using the country of the employee’s company (not their nationality). It installs automatically when both Employees and Phone Auto Format are installed.
